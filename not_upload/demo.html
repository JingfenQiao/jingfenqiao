Research

I am Jingfen Qiao, a Ph.D. student from the University of Amsterdam (UvA), supervised by Prof. dr. Evangelos Kanoulas and dr. Andrew Yates.

I believe that retrieval is the foundational pillar of Artificial Intelligence, serving as the critical bridge between static knowledge and dynamic reasoning. My research aims to build highly effective, scalable, and robust search systems that empower the next generation of AI through architectural innovations in Agentic Retrieval/Reranking and Efficient Representation Learning.
