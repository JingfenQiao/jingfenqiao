Research

I am Jingfen Qiao, a PhD Candidate in Information Retrieval at the University of Amsterdam (UvA), supervised by Prof. dr. Evangelos Kanoulas and Dr. Andrew Yates. My research aims to build highly effective, scalable, and robust search systems that empower the next generation of AI through architectural innovations in Agentic Retrieval/Reranking and Efficient Representation Learning.
